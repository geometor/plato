Mathematical & Geometric Dialogues¶

Loci Mathematici et Geometrici — Plato on Geometry, Proportion & Noetic Ascent

ΠΛΑΤΩΝΟΣ ΔΙΑΛΟΓΟΙ ΜΑΘΗΜΑΤΙΚΟΙ
Loci Mathematici et Geometrici

Author: Plato of Athens (c. 428–348 BC)
Critical Greek Edition: John Burnet (Platonis Opera, Oxford Classical Texts, 1900–1907)
English Translation: Benjamin Jowett (3rd ed., Oxford University Press, 1892)
GEOMETOR Digital Edition: Synchronized English, Greek OCT, Summary & Notes Tracks

Mathematical & Geometric Dialogues (Loci Mathematici et Geometrici) unites into a single critical Digital Scriptorium edition the eleven foundational mathematical and geometric passages across seven dialogues of Plato of Athens (Meno, Theaetetus, Republic VI–VII, Timaeus, Parmenides, Philebus, and Laws VII) that established deductive hypothesis, quadratic powers, the Divided Line and Quadrivium, harmonic proportion, the five cosmic polyhedra, and Limit.

Every unit is presented across four synchronized tracks—English (Benjamin Jowett's public-domain 3rd edition, 1892), Greek (Burnet OCT) (John Burnet's Platonis Opera, 1900–1907), Summary, and Critical Notes (pairing interactive .euclid-graphic SVG diagrams and literal Greek mathematical philology with reception across Euclid, Proclus, Aristotle, Pacioli, and Hoenen).
