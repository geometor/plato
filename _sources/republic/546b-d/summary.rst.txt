===========================================
The Nuptial Number — Synopsis
===========================================

.. rubric:: The Speech of the Muses & the Platonic Geometric Number · Republic VIII, 546b–d

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

In *Republic* VIII, Socrates invokes the tragic irony of the Muses to explain why even the ideal state will eventually decay: everything generated by time is subject to corruption. Human fecundity and degenerate births are governed by a master **geometrical number** (*arithmos geōmetrikos*, :ref:`546d <stephanus-republic-546d>`):

1. **Divine vs. Human Period** (`546b`): That of divine birth is contained in a **perfect number** (*teleios arithmos*); that of human birth is comprehended in a number combining squaring and cubing (*auxēseis dynamenai te kai dynasteuomenai*).
2. **The 3:4:5 Triad & Four Harmonies** (`546c`): The sesquitertian base (:math:`4:3`) paired with :math:`5` (*epitritos pythmen pempadi syzygeis*) furnishes two harmonies when cubed:
   - **First Harmony**: An equilateral square of :math:`100 \times 4 = 400` (or :math:`100 \times 36 = 3600`).
   - **Second Harmony**: An oblong of :math:`100` numbers on rational diameters of :math:`5` (where :math:`7^2 - 1 = 48`, giving :math:`4800`), or two irrational diameters (:math:`50 - 2 = 48`), and :math:`100` cubes of :math:`3` (:math:`27 \times 100 = 2700`).
3. **The Total Geometric Sum**: The sum :math:`400 + 4900 + 2700 = 8000` (or :math:`20^3 = 8000`, or :math:`(3 \times 4 \times 5)^4 = 12,960,000` under James Adam's reconstruction) controls the generation of noble and base souls.
