Proclus Diadochus
=================

**Neoplatonic Diadochus & Commentator on Euclid I and Plato's Timaeus**

Wove Philebus's Limit/Unlimited, Republic's Divided Line (dianoia), Meno's anamnesis, and Timaeus's polyhedra into the Prologue of his Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements.
