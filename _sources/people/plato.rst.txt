Plato of Athens
===============

**Founder of the Academy & Architect of Mathematical Philosophy**

Founded the Athenian Academy (c. 387 BC), elevating pure arithmetic, plane geometry, solid geometry, mathematical astronomy, and harmonics into the indispensable propaedeutic to dialectic and catalyzing the research of Theaetetus and Eudoxus.
