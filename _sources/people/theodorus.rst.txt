Theodorus of Cyrene
===================

**Pythagorean Geometer & Teacher of Plato and Theaetetus**

Proved case-by-case via diagrams that the sides of squares of 3, 5, ..., 17 square feet are incommensurable in length with the 1-foot unit (Theaetetus 147d).
