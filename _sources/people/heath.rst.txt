Sir Thomas Little Heath
=======================

**Historian of Greek Mathematics & Editor of Euclid's Elements**

Author of A History of Greek Mathematics (1921) and The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements (1926), reconstructing Meno 86e–87b, Theodorus's proofs, and Timaeus's astronomy.
