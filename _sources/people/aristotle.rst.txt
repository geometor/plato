Aristotle of Stagira
====================

**Student of Plato & Author of the Analytics, Physics, De Caelo & Metaphysics**

Preserves vital testimony on Plato's mathematical intermediates (ta mathēmatika), resolves Meno's paradox in Posterior Analytics I.1, and critiques the Timaeus planar-triangle physics in De Caelo III.7–8.
