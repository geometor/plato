St. Thomas Aquinas
==================

**Scholastic Doctor of Mathematical Abstraction & Judgment**

Synthesized Platonic-Aristotelian mathematical epistemology through abstraction in intelligible matter and resolution to imagination (resolutio ad imaginationem).
