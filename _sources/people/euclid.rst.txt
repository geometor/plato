Euclid of Alexandria
====================

**Author of the Thirteen Books of Elements**

Systematized the geometric heritage of Plato's Academy, culminating in Book X (Theaetetus's irrationals) and Book XIII (construction and uniqueness of the five Platonic cosmic polyhedra, praised by Proclus as the telos of the Elements).
