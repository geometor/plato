Pythagoras & the Italic Pythagoreans
====================================

**Pioneers of Harmonic Ratios, Figurate Numbers & Application of Areas**

Discovered the diatonic ratios (2:1, 3:2, 4:3, 9:8, 256:243), the application of areas, and quadratic incommensurability; commended in Republic VII 530d as treating astronomy and harmonics as sister sciences.
