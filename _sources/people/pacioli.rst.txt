Fra Luca Pacioli
================

**Renaissance Mathematician & Author of De Divina Proportione (1509)**

Celebrated Timaeus 53c–56c across nine chapters of De Divina Proportione, pairing Leonardo da Vinci's skeletal polyhedral plates with Euclid Book XIII and the golden ratio.
