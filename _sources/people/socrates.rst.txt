Socrates of Athens
==================

**Dialectical Interlocutor in Meno, Theaetetus, Republic & Philebus**

Guides Meno's attendant through the diagonal doubling proof, frames Theaetetus's classification of powers, expounds the Divided Line and Quadrivium, and analyzes Limit (peras) and pure geometric beauty in the Philebus.
