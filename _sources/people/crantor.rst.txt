Crantor of Soli
===============

**Old Academic First Exegete of Plato's Timaeus**

Introduced the lambda (Λ) arrangement of the doubles (1, 2, 4, 8) and triples (1, 3, 9, 27) and the least integer base 384 clearing all fractions of the 34-term World-Soul diatonic scale.
