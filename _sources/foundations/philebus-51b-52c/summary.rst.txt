===========================================
Pure Beauty — Synopsis
===========================================

.. rubric:: Intrinsic Mathematical Aesthetics · Philebus 51b–52c

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

2. **Limit (*Peras*), Unlimited (*Apeiron*) & Pure Constructive Beauty (*Philebus* 16c–26d, 51b–52c)**: At `51b–52c`, Socrates defines **pure, non-relative beauty**: surfaces and solids constructed cleanly by :term:`compass and straightedge <tornos-kanon>` (*τόρνοις… καὶ κανόσι*).
