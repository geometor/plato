===========================================
Limit & Unlimited — Synopsis
===========================================

.. rubric:: Peras, Apeiron & the Ontology of Number · Philebus 16c–26d

Architectural Thesis
--------------------

In *Philebus* `16c–26d`, :person:`Socrates <socrates>` establishes :term:`Limit <peras>` (*πέρας*—the equal, double, and every exact ratio of number to number or measure to measure) and the :term:`Unlimited <apeiron>` (*ἄπειρον*—the indeterminate continuum of more and less) as the universal principles of mathematical order, adopted verbatim by :person:`Proclus <proclus>` at the head of his *Euclid* commentary.
