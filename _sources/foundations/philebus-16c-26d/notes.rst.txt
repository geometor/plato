===========================================
Limit & Unlimited — Critical Notes
===========================================

.. rubric:: Mathematical Ontology & Neoplatonic Geometry · Philebus 16c–26d

I. Literal Greek Mathematical Philology (*Contra Paraphrasim*)
---------------------------------------------------------------

- **Philebus 25a7–b1 — ὁπόσα… ἀριθμὸν πρὸς ἀριθμὸν ἢ μέτρον πρὸς μέτρον**: Notice Plato's deliberate disjunction: **number to number** (*ἀριθμὸς πρὸς ἀριθμόν*, commensurable rational ratios of discrete arithmetic) **or measure to measure** (*μέτρον πρὸς μέτρον*, continuous geometric ratios encompassing incommensurable magnitudes via :person:`Eudoxus's <eudoxus>` Book V theory of proportion!).

II. Reception
-------------

- **Proclus** (:cite:`In Euclidem Prologue I, Friedlein 5.18–7.12 <proclus:in-euclidem>`; ``plato:philebus:16c-26d``): Limit (*peras*) and Unlimited (*apeiron*) are the supreme ontological principles of geometry, governing points and lines, figures and boundless space.
