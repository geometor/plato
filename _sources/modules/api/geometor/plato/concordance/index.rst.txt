geometor.plato.concordance
==========================

.. py:module:: geometor.plato.concordance

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Stephanus concordance and SHA-256 primary-source manifest for *geometor-plato*.

   Reconciles every ``plato:*`` citation across the GEOMETOR canon (``euclid``,
   ``elements``, ``proclus``, ``aristotle``, ``hoenen``, and ``pacioli`` — 58
   repository-level citation entries across 63 apparatus files, 33 distinct
   normalized citation keys) against canonical Stephanus anchors in the six units of
   *Loci Mathematici et Geometrici*, and records cryptographic SHA-256 checksums for
   all 14 public-domain Burnet (1900–1907) and Jowett (1892) source texts.

.. py:class:: ConcordanceEntry

   Canonical cross-corpus Plato citation mapping to Stephanus anchors.

   :ivar cite_key: Exact citation key used in sister repositories (e.g., ``"plato:meno:76A"``).
   :ivar repos: Tuple of sister repositories declaring this key.
   :ivar dialogue: Canonical Platonic dialogue title.
   :ivar stephanus_locus: Normalized Stephanus coordinate or span.
   :ivar in_codex: ``True`` when staged verbatim in Units I–VI; ``False`` for peripheral
                   civic/mimetic passages catalogued in Critical Notes.
   :ivar unit_slug: Target unit folder slug in ``docsrc/loci_mathematici/`` (or nearest note unit).
   :ivar stephanus_anchor: Exact RST target label ``stephanus-<dialogue>-<sec>`` (when ``in_codex``).
   :ivar euclid_proclus_context: Mathematical/philosophical locus in Euclid, Proclus, Aristotle,
                                 Hoenen, or Pacioli.
