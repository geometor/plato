geometor.plato.constructions¶

Exact symbolic constructions for Plato’s Mathematical & Geometric Dialogues.

Every construction builds a Model with auto_intersect=False and 100% exact SymPy coordinates over \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\), or closed-form quadratic radicals—never injecting float or sp.Float approximations.

Constructions provided:

  1. construct_meno_diagonal_doubling() (Meno 82b–85b): The \(2\times 2\) given square (\(Q=4\)), false \(4\times 4\) (\(Q=16\)) and \(3\times 3\) (\(Q=9\)) conjectures, and Socrates’ inscribed diagonal square (\(Q=8\)).

  2. construct_theodorus_spiral() (Theaetetus 147c–148b): Pure straightedge-and-compass right-triangle spiral constructing exact quadrances \(Q(O, P_n) = n\) for \(n = 1, 2, \dots, 17\), classifying lengths (mēkē) vs. powers (dynameis).

  3. construct_divided_line() (Republic VI, 509d–511e): Exact division of the Visible and Intelligible realms proving Q(pistis) == Q(dianoia) identically, with semicircle geometric-mean ordinates and Golden Ratio default.

  4. construct_timaeus_proportion_circles() (Timaeus 31c–36d): Semicircle construction of Plato’s arithmetic (\(3\)), geometric (\(2\sqrt{2}\)), and harmonic (\(8/3\)) means on the \([2, 4]\) octave, exhibiting the \(9:8\) epogdoos tone and the inclined circles of the Same and the Different.

  5. construct_timaeus_triangles() & construct_timaeus_cube_face() (Timaeus 53c–56c): Six \(1:\sqrt{3}:2\) scalene half-triangles (quadrances \(1, 3, 4\); spreads \(1/4, 3/4, 1\)) around centroid \(G = X(2)\) forming the cosmic equilateral face, plus the 4-isosceles-triangle square face of the Cube.

  6. construct_compass_and_peras() (Parmenides 137e, Philebus 51c, Laws VII 819d): Circular equidistance (tornos), optical straightness (kanōn), and the \(1, \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}\) length–breadth–depth incommensurability chain.

geometor.plato.constructions.construct_meno_diagonal_doubling(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct Socrates’ diagonal square-doubling proof (Meno 82b–85b).

Starting from the unit/two-foot baseline A=(0,0), U=(1,0), B=(2,0), constructs by straightedge and compass:

  • Initial \(2\times 2\) square < A B C D > of side \(2\) (\(Q(A, B) = 4\), area \(4\)),

  • First false conjecture < A E F G > of doubled side \(4\) (\(Q(A, E) = 16\), area \(16\)),

  • Second false conjecture < A M N P > of three-foot side \(3\) (\(Q(A, M) = 9\), area \(9\)),

  • Inscribed diagonal square < B H K D > cut by the four diametroi (\(Q(B, H) = 8\), area \(8\)).

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model (created if None).

Returns:

Populated Model with verified assertions.

geometor.plato.constructions.classify_theaetetus_powers(max_n: int = 17) → list[TheaetetusPowerEntry]¶

Classify integer areas 1..max_n into mēkē (lengths) and dynameis (powers).

Follows Theaetetus’s exact dichotomy (Theaetetus 147e–148b):

  • Equilateral square numbers (isos isakis, \(n = k^2\)) produce lines commensurable in length with the unit (mēkē).

  • Oblong numbers (promēkeis, non-square \(n\)) produce lines incommensurable in length with the unit, commensurable only in the plane areas they have the power to form (dynameis).

Parameters:

max_n – Upper integer area bound (defaults to Theodorus’s 17).

Returns:

List of TheaetetusPowerEntry records for 1..max_n.

geometor.plato.constructions.construct_theodorus_spiral(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, max_n: int = 17) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct Theodorus’s exact \(\sqrt{1}\dots\sqrt{17}\) spiral (Theaetetus 147d–148b).

Starting from O=(0,0) and P1=(1,0), each vertex P_{n+1} is erected along the perpendicular to [O, P_n] at P_n (Euclid I.11) at exact unit step Q(P_n, P_{n+1}) = 1 = Q(O, P1) (Postulate 3) in the positive orientation side_of=(O, P_n, +1), expanding cross-terms at each step so every coordinate remains a flat \(\mathbb{Q}\)-linear combination of \(\sqrt{k}\) (\(k \le n\)) with zero nested square roots and exact symbolic quadrance Q(O, P_{n+1}) = n + 1.

Parameters:

  • model – Optional existing Model.

  • max_n – Maximum integer area n (defaults to 17 per Theaetetus 147d6).

Returns:

Populated Model with vertices O, P1..P{max_n}.

geometor.plato.constructions.construct_divided_line(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, ratio: sympy.Expr = sp.GoldenRatio) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct Plato’s Divided Line (Republic VI, 509d–511e) and verify middle equality.

Divides baseline [A, E] at C into Visible AC and Intelligible CE in 1 : r, and subdivides each realm in the same ratio 1 : r at B and D:

  • AB (eikasia, length \(1\), \(Q = 1\)),

  • BC (pistis, length \(r\), \(Q = r^2\)),

  • CD (dianoia, length \(r\), \(Q = r^2\)),

  • DE (noēsis, length \(r^2\), \(Q = r^4\)).

Also constructs the semicircle on [A, K] of length \(1 + r^2\) (AB + DE) whose ordinate BG at B has exact length \(\sqrt{1 \cdot r^2} = r\), demonstrating geometrically why BC = CD = BG.

Parameters:

  • model – Optional existing Model.

  • ratio – Symbolic proportion \(r > 0\) (defaults to sp.GoldenRatio).

Returns:

Populated Model with verified equal-middle proof.

geometor.plato.constructions.construct_timaeus_proportion_circles(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct the Three Platonic Means & Circles of Same/Different (Timaeus 31c–36d).

On diameter AB of length \(2 + 4 = 6\) (A=(0,0), J=(2,0), O=(3,0), B=(6,0)), exhibits in a single exact Euclidean figure:

  • Arithmetic mean O G of length \(3 = \frac{2+4}{2}\) (hēmiolios, \(Q = 9\)),

  • Geometric mean J G of length \(2\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{2\cdot 4}\) (\(Q = 8\)),

  • Harmonic mean G H of length \(\frac{8}{3} = \frac{2\cdot 2\cdot 4}{2+4}\) (epitritos, \(Q = 64/9\)),

  • Epogdoos ratio \(\frac{OG}{GH} = \frac{3}{8/3} = \frac{9}{8}\) (\(Q(O,G)/Q(G,H) = 81/64\)),

together with the equatorial Circle of the Same (O, B) and the inclined Circle of the Different [P, Q] crossing at O like the Greek letter \(\mathrm{X}\) (chi, 36b–c).

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated Model.

Construct Unit 6’s Compass (Tornos), Straight Ray (Kanōn) & \(1,\sqrt{2},\sqrt{3}\) Triad.

Synthesizes the three foundational definitions and problems of Unit 6:

  1. Parmenides 137e & Philebus 51c: Circle (O, A) whose extremities A, B, C are equidistant from the middle O=(0,0), and straight line [A, O, C] whose middle O stands optically before both extremes.

  2. Laws VII, 819d–820d: Unit length OC (\(Q = 1\)), breadth CD (\(Q = 1\)) yielding plane diagonal OD (\(Q = 2\)), and unfolded unit depth DE perpendicular to OD yielding solid cube diagonal OE (\(Q = 3\)).
