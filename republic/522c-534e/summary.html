The Quadrivium & Dialectic — Synopsis¶

The Five Mathematical Disciplines of the Philosopher-King · Republic VII, 525a–534e

Architectural Thesis¶

Unit III unites the two monumental architects of Western mathematical epistemology—Book VI’s Divided Line (509d–511e) and Book VII’s Curriculum of the Five Mathematical Sciences & Dialectic (525a–534e):

  1. The Five Sciences of the Quadrivium & the Lament on Stereometry (VII, 525a–534e): To turn the soul from becoming to eternal being (525b–527b), Socrates orders five ascending sciences: (i) Arithmetic & Logistic (525a–526c), (ii) Plane Geometry (526c–527c), (iii) Solid Geometry (528a–d—inserting his prophetic lament that three-dimensional stereometry remains neglected and unguided by cities), (iv) Mathematical Astronomy (528e–530c), and (v) Pythagorean Harmonics of Ratios (530d–531c), crowned by Dialectic (531d–534e).
