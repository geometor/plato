The Nuptial Number — Synopsis¶

The Speech of the Muses & the Platonic Geometric Number · Republic VIII, 546b–d

Architectural Thesis¶

In Republic VIII, Socrates invokes the tragic irony of the Muses to explain why even the ideal state will eventually decay: everything generated by time is subject to corruption. Human fecundity and degenerate births are governed by a master geometrical number (arithmos geōmetrikos, 546d):

  1. Divine vs. Human Period (546b): That of divine birth is contained in a perfect number (teleios arithmos); that of human birth is comprehended in a number combining squaring and cubing (auxēseis dynamenai te kai dynasteuomenai).

  2. The 3:4:5 Triad & Four Harmonies (546c): The sesquitertian base (\(4:3\)) paired with \(5\) (epitritos pythmen pempadi syzygeis) furnishes two harmonies when cubed: - First Harmony: An equilateral square of \(100 \times 4 = 400\) (or \(100 \times 36 = 3600\)). - Second Harmony: An oblong of \(100\) numbers on rational diameters of \(5\) (where \(7^2 - 1 = 48\), giving \(4800\)), or two irrational diameters (\(50 - 2 = 48\)), and \(100\) cubes of \(3\) (\(27 \times 100 = 2700\)).

  3. The Total Geometric Sum: The sum \(400 + 4900 + 2700 = 8000\) (or \(20^3 = 8000\), or \((3 \times 4 \times 5)^4 = 12,960,000\) under James Adam’s reconstruction) controls the generation of noble and base souls.
