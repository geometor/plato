Extremes & Figure — Synopsis¶

Whole, Parts, Limit & Figure · Parmenides 145a–b

Architectural Thesis¶

In the Second Hypothesis of the Parmenides, Plato deduces that whatever is a whole composed of parts must possess limits (perata), extremes (eschata), and a beginning, middle, and end—thereby grounding the necessity of geometric figure (schēma).
