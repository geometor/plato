Παρμενίδης (145a–b) — VI.2 Beginning, Middle, Extremes & Figure: Second Hypothesis (Parm. 145a–b)¶

Platonis Opera, Tomus II (Oxford, 1901; 2nd ed. 1910) · tlg0059.tlg009

f. Parm. 145b
