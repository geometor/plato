Limit & Unlimited — Synopsis¶

Peras, Apeiron & the Ontology of Number · Philebus 16c–26d

Architectural Thesis¶

In Philebus 16c–26d, Socrates establishes Limit (πέρας—the equal, double, and every exact ratio of number to number or measure to measure) and the Unlimited (ἄπειρον—the indeterminate continuum of more and less) as the universal principles of mathematical order, adopted verbatim by Proclus at the head of his Euclid commentary.
