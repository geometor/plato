==============================================================================
Loci Mathematici et Geometrici — Plato on Geometry, Proportion & Noetic Ascent
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Synchronized 4-Track Book Codex (English · Greek Burnet OCT · Summary · Critical Notes)

**Loci Mathematici et Geometrici** unites into a single critical Digital Scriptorium edition the eleven foundational mathematical and geometric passages across seven dialogues of :person:`Plato of Athens <plato>` (*Meno*, *Theaetetus*, *Republic* VI–VII, *Timaeus*, *Parmenides*, *Philebus*, and *Laws* VII) that established deductive hypothesis, quadratic :term:`powers <dynamis>`, the :term:`Divided Line <dianoia>` and Quadrivium, harmonic :term:`proportion <analogia>`, the five cosmic polyhedra, and :term:`Limit <peras>`.

Every unit is presented across **four synchronized tracks**—**English** (:person:`Benjamin Jowett's <jowett>` public-domain 3rd edition, 1892), **Greek (Burnet OCT)** (:person:`John Burnet's <burnet>` *Platonis Opera*, 1900–1907), **Summary**, and **Critical Notes** (pairing interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG diagrams and literal Greek mathematical philology with reception across :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, :person:`Proclus <proclus>`, :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`, :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>`, and :person:`Hoenen <hoenen>`).
