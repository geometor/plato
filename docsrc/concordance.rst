============================
Master Stephanus Concordance
============================

.. rubric:: Cross-Corpus Reconciliation Across Euclid, Elements, Proclus, Aristotle, Hoenen & Pacioli

This concordance reconciles every ``plato:*`` citation across the six sister repositories of the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium (**36 canonical citation keys** spanning **58 repository-level declarations** and **63 apparatus files** in ``geometor/euclid``, ``geometor/elements``, ``geometor/proclus``, ``geometor/aristotle``, ``geometor/hoenen``, and ``geometor/pacioli``) directly to synchronized Stephanus anchors in *Loci Mathematici et Geometrici*.
