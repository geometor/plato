Eudoxus of Cnidus
=================

**Academic Geometer of General Proportion & Method of Exhaustion**

Answered Plato's challenges with the general theory of proportion valid for commensurable and incommensurable magnitudes alike (Euclid Book V) and rigorous stereometric volume theorems (Euclid Book XII).
