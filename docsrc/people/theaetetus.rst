Theaetetus of Athens
====================

**Academic Geometer of Irrational Powers & the Five Regular Solids**

Generalized Theodorus's surds into the universal classification of lengths (mēkē) and powers (dynameis), founded the theory of medial/binomial/apotome irrationals (Euclid Book X), and first constructed all five regular polyhedra (Euclid Book XIII).
