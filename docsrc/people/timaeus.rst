Timaeus of Locri)
=================

**Italic Pythagorean Astronomer-Statesman & Speaker of the Timaeus**

Expounds the geometric proportion binding the four elements, the harmonic division of the World-Soul (31b–36d), and the reconstruction of physical bodies from elementary isosceles and scalene right triangles (53c–56c).
