Method of Hypothesis — Synopsis¶

Inquiry from Hypothesis & Application of Area · Meno 86e–87b

Architectural Thesis¶

Unit I gathers the three foundational mathematical episodes of Plato's Meno—the earliest surviving Greek text to stage an explicit geometric diagram step by step:

  1. Inquiry from Hypothesis & Application of Area (86e–87b): Pressed to ask whether virtue is teachable prior to knowing its essence, Socrates adopts the geometer’s method of hypothesis (ἐξ ὑποθέσεως, 86e): reducing the inscription of a triangular area inside a given circle to an elliptic application of area along the diameter.
